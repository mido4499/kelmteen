Kelmteen Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Kelmteen is made by Ahmed Shaheen ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. In short: we collect only what's needed for you to send notes to your friends, we don't sell your data, and we don't show ads or track you across other apps or websites.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Phone numberTo sign you in with a text code, and so friends who have your number can find you.
Name, profile photo and assigned colorSo your friends know who a note is from.
Friends, invites and blocksTo know who can send you notes and who can't.
Notes you send and receiveTo deliver them to your friend's widget.
Reports you file, or that others file about youTo keep Kelmteen safe. Reports include the reported person's name, number and photo at the time.

2. Your contacts

If you allow access, the app reads the phone numbers in your contacts to show which of them use Kelmteen. Numbers are turned into codes (hashes) on your phone before being checked, and we don't store your contacts. When you invite someone, we keep a code made from their number so we can show them your invite if they join; it's deleted once they accept, decline, or delete their account.

3. Who can see your information

  • Your friends can see your name, photo, color and phone number, and the notes you send them.
  • Anyone with your invite link can see your name, photo and color.
  • People who have your number in their contacts can see that you're on Kelmteen, with your name, photo and color.
  • We can access your data to run the service and review reports.

4. Services we use

These companies process data for us only to provide Kelmteen:

  • Supabase: stores accounts, friends, notes and photos (servers in the United States).
  • Twilio: sends sign-in codes to your phone number.
  • Cloudflare: hosts this website and invite links.
  • Resend: emails us when someone files a report.
  • Apple: delivers notifications and widget updates.

5. How long we keep it

Notes show on the widget for about a day. Everything else is kept until you delete it or your account. We may keep information about reports as long as needed to keep Kelmteen safe, unless you delete your account.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app at any time: Me → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and its data: your profile, photo, friends, invites, notes, blocks, and reports filed by or about you. You can also remove your photo by choosing a new one, remove friends, and block people at any time.

7. Children

Kelmteen isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to how it's used. Email us and we'll help.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll let you know in the app.
