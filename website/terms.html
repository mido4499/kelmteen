2. Zero tolerance for objectionable content

Kelmteen is for being kind to your friends. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. You may not use Kelmteen, including your name, profile photo and notes, to:

If you break these rules we may remove your content, suspend your account, or delete it, without warning.

3. Reporting and blocking

You can block anyone from their page in the app. Blocking ends the friendship and stops them from adding you; they aren't told. You can also report someone from their page, from their invite, or from your Blocked list. Reports are private: the person you report is not told who reported them. We review reports promptly and aim to act within 24 hours.

4. Your content

Your notes, name and photo stay yours. You give us permission to store them and show them to the friends you choose, only so Kelmteen can work. You're responsible for what you send. Notes are meant to be short-lived: they show on your friend's widget for about a day.

5. Privacy

Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and how it's used. In short:

  • We use your phone number to sign you in and so friends who have it can find you.
  • To show which contacts use Kelmteen, the app turns their phone numbers into codes (hashes) on your phone before checking them. We don't keep your contacts.
  • When you invite someone, we keep a code made from their number so we can offer your invite if they join.
  • Your profile photo is visible to your friends.

6. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Me → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and its data. We may suspend or end accounts that break these terms.
